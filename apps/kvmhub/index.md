# KVMHub

KVMHub is a local-network keyboard and pointer sharing utility for Mac.

## Documents

- [Privacy policy]({{ "/apps/kvmhub/privacy/" | relative_url }})
- [Support]({{ "/apps/kvmhub/support/" | relative_url }})

## App Summary

- Arrange nearby computers beside your Mac displays and move through a shared
  edge to transfer keyboard and pointer control.
- Pair computers with a matching six-digit code and authenticate future
  connections with their saved identities.
- Send encrypted control traffic directly over the local network without an
  account or developer-operated cloud service.
